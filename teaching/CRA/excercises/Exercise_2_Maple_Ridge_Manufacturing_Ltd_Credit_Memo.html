1.Purpose and summary

Maple Ridge has asked the Bank to take over its banking from Lakeshore Commercial Bank, which is exiting after a FY2026 covenant breach (DSCR 1.10x against 1.25x), and to finance a C$1.8M printing line. The business has grown sales 26% in two years, but normalized EBITDA fell to $590k (margin 7.6%). After tax, CapEx and dividends, 2026 debt service was covered only 0.42x. HST and payroll remittances are overdue and the owners have taken out cash through dividends and advances.

As requested, pro forma leverage would be 6.23x EBITDA and downside lender DSCR 0.75x. The line is larger than the borrowing base (0.82x), and term debt is 67% covered by equipment value. We do not support the request as proposed. A reduced facility that refinances the existing debt and provides a margined line is serviceable from current earnings (1.11x lender DSCR, 1.81x on EBITDA) if dividends stop and the conditions below are met.

3.Borrower and management

Printed packaging manufacturer (46 employees), owned by Daniel Okafor (60%, age 66) and Elena Petrova (40%, CEO). Customer X makes up 31% of revenue; its contract ends in 10 months, and it is asking for a 4% price cut and 60-day terms. The plant is rented month-to-month from MRM Properties Inc., which the shareholders own. Okafor plans to sell his shares to Petrova for about $1.8M from 2028. The statements are compiled, and the finance function is a part-time controller.

11.Recommendation

We recommend declining the request as proposed and authorizing the RM to present a counter-offer of a $1,000,000 operating line (margined as above) and a $1,240,000 refinance loan (6-year amortization, 7.25%), for total authorized credit of $2,240,000. Approval is subject to all conditions precedent being met before closing. The $1,500,000 equipment loan is declined at this time. It may be resubmitted with a signed Customer X renewal, two quarters of results showing recovery, at least $500k of verified owner equity, and the MRM Properties security in place. Proposed borrower risk rating: 7 – Special mention.
