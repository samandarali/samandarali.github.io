Daily Commercial Credit Practice · Exercise 2 · Advanced

Maple Ridge Manufacturing Ltd.

A takeover request from a borrower whose current lender wants out. Three facilities, related-party arrangements, priority claims and an expiring major contract. Decide what — if anything — the bank should approve.

ApproveApprove with ConditionsDecline / Counter-offer
Your role: You are the credit analyst at a new lender being asked to take over this relationship. Unlike Exercise 1, the statements do not give EBITDA, and several items must be adjusted before ratios mean anything. Analyse each facility separately, then recommend a decision and structure. Try the case yourself first, then compare with the model solution: the credit analysis, the model dashboard and the credit memo (steps 2–4).

9Related-Party and Ownership Matters

  • The plant is rented from MRM Properties at $252k a year on a month-to-month basis; there is no written lease.
  • "Due from shareholders" ($180k) are interest-free advances to Daniel Okafor with no repayment terms.
  • Dividends rose from $120k to $180k while net income fell.
  • Daniel Okafor plans to retire and sell his 60% to Elena Petrova over three years from 2028 for about $1.8M. The intention is to fund it through company dividends or a share redemption.
  • The financial statements are compiled (Notice to Reader), not reviewed or audited.

10Management, Operations and Market

Positive information

  • 12-year history; experienced CEO with a sales background.
  • Revenue has grown every year (+26% over two years).
  • Strong plant utilization; new line replaces outsourced printing.
  • Food-packaging demand is relatively stable.

Concerns

  • Customer X is asking for a 4% price reduction to renew.
  • About 35% of material purchases are priced in US dollars; there is no hedging.
  • Paperboard and resin prices are volatile; there are no price-adjustment clauses with most customers.
  • Covenant breach and forced exit from the incumbent bank.
  • Overdue HST and payroll remittances.
  • No independent finance function; the controller is part-time.

Management assumptions

  • Customer X renews at current pricing for three years.
  • Full $330k of savings from mid-February 2027.
  • Volume growth of 14% with no change in the exchange rate or material prices.

Lender downside

Customer X renews with a 4% price reduction, revenue is 10% below management's projection, and only 50% of savings are achieved. Under these assumptions, 2027 EBITDA would be about $690,000.

Your Credit-Report Assignment

Prepare a formal credit report and decide on each facility separately. In particular:

  1. Calculate EBITDA, normalized for non-recurring items, and EBITDAR.
  2. Calculate liquidity, efficiency, leverage, coverage and profitability ratios.
  3. Calculate adjusted tangible net worth (deduct goodwill and amounts due from shareholders).
  4. Calculate fixed-charge coverage including rent, and DSCR on historical, pro forma and downside bases.
  5. Build a pro forma balance sheet and 2027 cash flow after the takeover and equipment purchase.
  6. Run sensitivity analysis: revenue, savings, Customer X pricing, the exchange rate, interest rates and loss of Customer X.
  7. Calculate a borrowing base with priority-payables reserves and a concentration limit, and test the $1.4M line.
  8. Assess the collateral shortfall and whether MRM Properties should support the credit.
  9. Explain what the incumbent lender's actions tell you.
  10. Recommend: approve, approve with conditions, or decline with a counter-offer, including structure, covenants and conditions precedent.
