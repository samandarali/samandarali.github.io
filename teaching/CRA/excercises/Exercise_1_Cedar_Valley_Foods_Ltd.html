Customer concentration

  • Customer C: 11% of revenue

2Financing Request and Purpose

  1. $1,200,000 six-year term loan to purchase and install an automated packaging line costing $1,500,000.
  2. Increase the operating line from $700,000 to $1,100,000.

The borrower will contribute $300,000 from company cash toward the equipment purchase.

Management's objectives

  • Increase production capacity by 20%.
  • Reduce packaging labour costs by approximately $240,000 annually.
  • Reduce product waste and support a new contract with Customer A.
  • Complete installation in four months, with partial production interruption for approximately three weeks.

3Proposed Credit Structure

Term loan

  • Amount: $1,200,000
  • Amortization: six years
  • Interest rate: 7.0%
  • Annual payments: $251,700
  • First-ranking charge over new equipment

Operating line

  • Requested limit: $1,100,000
  • Rate: prime + 1.75%
  • General security agreement
  • 75% of eligible A/R under 90 days
  • 50% of eligible inventory

10Management and Operations

Positive information

  • Priya Sharma has managed the company for 14 years.
  • No missed scheduled loan payments.
  • Product demand has grown steadily.
  • Experienced operations manager.
  • New Customer A contract has a three-year initial term.

Concerns

  • EBITDA declined despite revenue growth.
  • Gross margin declined.
  • A/R and inventory grew faster than revenue.
  • Significant customer concentration.
  • Customer A may terminate for performance failures.
  • Installation delays could affect production.
  • No formal succession plan.

Projection assumptions

  • Full realization of $240,000 in annual labour savings.
  • No material installation delays.
  • Successful completion of the new Customer A contract.
  • Gross-margin improvement and no increase in raw-material prices.

Lender downside scenario

Revenue is 10% below management's projection and only 50% of expected labour savings are achieved. Under these assumptions, 2027 EBITDA would be approximately $760,000.

Your Credit-Report Assignment

Prepare a formal report covering:

  1. Borrower and business overview
  2. Financing request and purpose
  3. Historical financial performance
  4. Liquidity and working capital
  5. Leverage and capitalization
  6. Cash flow and debt-service capacity
  7. Projections and downside scenario
  8. Primary and secondary repayment sources
  9. Collateral and line availability
  10. Key risks
  11. Risk mitigants
  12. Recommended structure and conditions
  13. Final recommendation

Calculate the relevant ratios yourself. Also determine whether the requested operating line is adequately supported after excluding ineligible receivables and slow-moving inventory.

Final recommendation: Approve / Approve with Conditions / Decline

Support your decision with repayment capacity, risks, mitigants, security, and proposed conditions.
