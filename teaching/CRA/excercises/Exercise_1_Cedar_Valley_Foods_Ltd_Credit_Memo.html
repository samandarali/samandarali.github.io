1.Purpose and summary

We request approval of a new $1,200,000 equipment term loan to finance 80% of a $1,500,000 automated packaging line, and an increase in the operating line from $700,000 to $1,100,000 to fund working capital for the growth the new line supports, including a new three-year contract with the borrower's largest customer.

The borrower has a 14-year operating history, an experienced owner-manager and no missed payments. However, FY2026 results show falling margins (EBITDA margin 7.4%), lengthening working-capital cycles and negative free cash flow of $190k after distributions. On FY2026 earnings, the historical business cannot carry the new debt (lender DSCR 0.84x). Repayment therefore relies on the project delivering labour savings and on distributions being stopped. The operating line is well covered by the borrowing base (1.45x). The term loan is under-secured by $375k. We support the request with a modified structure and the conditions below.

3.Borrower and management

Cedar Valley Foods manufactures frozen meals for grocery chains, independent supermarkets and institutional buyers (58 employees). It is owned by Priya Sharma (70%), who has run the business since it started, and Arjun Sharma (30%). Three customers make up 52% of sales (A 24%, B 17%, C 11%). Management is experienced, but there is no formal succession plan.

Lender DSCR = (EBITDA − cash taxes − $140k maintenance CapEx − distributions) ÷ (term debt service + line interest), with distributions suspended. Term debt service as requested is $594.8k a year. The recommended structure (7-year amortization with 4 months interest-only) brings first-year service down to $519.4k. Coverage is acceptable on plan but thin in the downside. The remaining gap of about $50k a year at steady state can be absorbed by line availability while performance is monitored.

10.Terms and conditions

Conditions precedent to first advance

  1. Postponement and subordination of $150k shareholder loans.
  2. Evidence that the $300k equity contribution has been paid toward the equipment before the first advance.
  3. Executed equipment purchase contract, installation schedule and vendor warranty.
  4. Accountant's written reconciliation of FY2025 and FY2026 equity movements.
  5. Bank review of the Customer A contract (term, termination, performance standards, price pass-through).
  6. Resolution or exclusion of the $85k disputed receivable; inventory field exam before the increased line is available.
  7. Joint and several guarantees of the shareholders increased to $500k, with current personal net worth statements.
  8. Insurance assigned to the Bank (first loss payee), including business-interruption cover; key-person life insurance of $500k on the principal shareholder.

11.Recommendation

We recommend approval with conditions of the $1,200,000 equipment term loan (7-year amortization, 4 months interest-only during installation, 7.00% fixed) and the increase of the operating line to $1,100,000, margined as above, for a total connection of $3,930k. The borrower risk rating should move to 6 (Satisfactory / Watch) and be reviewed six months after the new line is commissioned. If FY2027 results fall below the lender downside case, the Bank should require an equity injection or a reduction in the line.
